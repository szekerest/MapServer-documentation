*****************************************************************************
 scalebar
*****************************************************************************

 .. note::

    The default scalebar measurement is cartesian: a straight-line distance
    measured on the map coordinate plane. Use ``MEASURE GEODESIC`` in the
    :ref:`SCALEBAR` object to request a local ellipsoidal ground-distance
    scalebar.

Purpose
-------

Creates a scalebar image from a mapfile. Output format of the image (such as 
PNG, JPEG, etc.) depends on your defined :ref:`IMAGETYPE <imagetype>` in 
your mapfile.
  
Syntax
------

:: 

    scalebar [mapfile] [output image]
